# DCF Team Adoption Playbook

A guide for rolling out the Dialectical Cognition Framework across your organization.

---

## Overview

Adopting DCF as a team is different from individual practice. This playbook provides a phased approach to organizational adoption, from initial pilot to full integration.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-2)

### Goals
- Identify champions
- Establish baseline
- Create initial awareness

### Actions

**1.1 Identify Champions (2-3 people)**

Look for team members who:
- Already engage thoughtfully with AI tools
- Are respected for their judgment
- Are willing to experiment publicly
- Can articulate what they learn

Champions will model behavior and help others adopt.

**1.2 Establish Baseline**

Before introducing DCF, assess current state:
- How does the team currently use AI?
- What's the quality of AI-assisted outputs?
- How often do people accept first responses vs. iterate?
- What frustrations exist with AI tools?

Document this. You'll compare later.

**1.3 Initial Exposure**

Share with champions:
- DCF Essentials (one-page overview)
- 2-3 Example Transcripts
- The core loop: Articulate → Generate → Evaluate → Refine

Don't overwhelm. Start simple.

### Deliverables
- [ ] Champions identified
- [ ] Baseline assessment documented
- [ ] Champions have read core materials

---

## Phase 2: Pilot (Weeks 3-4)

### Goals
- Champions practice DCF
- Gather initial evidence
- Identify friction points

### Actions

**2.1 Champion Practice**

Champions commit to:
- Using DCF in at least 3 meaningful AI interactions per week
- Documenting what works and what doesn't
- Sharing one "win" with the team each week

**2.2 Lightweight Rituals**

Introduce one practice team-wide:
- **Before approving AI outputs**: Ask "What assumptions are in this?"
- Start with just this one question
- Make it a team norm, not a rule

**2.3 Weekly Check-in**

Champions meet briefly (15 min) to share:
- What worked?
- What felt awkward?
- What questions came up?

### Deliverables
- [ ] Champions have 2+ weeks of practice
- [ ] One team-wide practice adopted
- [ ] Friction points documented

---

## Phase 3: Expansion (Weeks 5-8)

### Goals
- Broaden adoption beyond champions
- Introduce more practices
- Build shared vocabulary

### Actions

**3.1 Team Training Session**

Use the DCF Slide Deck to introduce the framework:
- 30-45 minute session
- Include live demonstration
- Share prompt library

**3.2 Add Checkpoint Protocol**

For code reviews and plan approvals, adopt the checkpoint questions:
1. What alternatives were considered?
2. What's the riskiest assumption?
3. What would make this fail?

Make these standard questions in review processes.

**3.3 Create Team CLAUDE.md**

Adapt the CLAUDE.md template for your team:
- Project context
- Team conventions
- DCF preferences (e.g., "Always present plans before implementing")

**3.4 Share Wins and Failures**

Create a channel or regular forum for:
- Sharing successful DCF interactions
- Discussing where DCF felt unhelpful
- Refining team practices

### Deliverables
- [ ] All team members introduced to DCF
- [ ] Checkpoint protocol in review processes
- [ ] Team CLAUDE.md created
- [ ] Sharing mechanism established

---

## Phase 4: Integration (Weeks 9-12)

### Goals
- DCF becomes default behavior
- Measure impact
- Refine practices

### Actions

**4.1 Measure Progress**

Compare to baseline:
- Quality of AI-assisted outputs
- Iteration patterns (first response vs. refined)
- Team satisfaction with AI tools
- Time spent on rework

**4.2 Refine Practices**

Based on experience, adjust:
- Which practices work for your team?
- Which feel like overhead?
- What's missing?

Don't keep practices that don't add value.

**4.3 Document Team Patterns**

Create team-specific resources:
- Prompt patterns that work for your domain
- Common failure modes you've encountered
- Adaptations to standard DCF practices

**4.4 Onboard New Members**

Create onboarding path for new team members:
1. Read DCF Essentials
2. Review team CLAUDE.md
3. Pair with a champion for first week
4. Observe team practices in action

### Deliverables
- [ ] Impact measured vs. baseline
- [ ] Practices refined based on experience
- [ ] Team-specific documentation created
- [ ] Onboarding process established

---

## Phase 5: Sustainability (Ongoing)

### Goals
- Maintain momentum
- Evolve practices
- Spread to other teams

### Actions

**5.1 Periodic Reflection**

Monthly or quarterly:
- What's working well?
- What's become rote/theatrical?
- What new practices should we try?

**5.2 Refresh Training**

Periodically revisit fundamentals:
- New team members need onboarding
- Practices drift without reinforcement
- New resources/frameworks emerge

**5.3 Cross-Team Sharing**

If successful, help other teams:
- Share your adapted materials
- Offer to run training sessions
- Document your adoption journey

### Deliverables
- [ ] Regular reflection cadence established
- [ ] Training refreshed periodically
- [ ] Cross-team sharing initiated

## Common Adoption Challenges

### "We don't have time for this"

**Response:**
- Start with ONE practice (the assumption question)
- DCF often saves time by reducing rework
- Measure time spent on rework before/after

### "This feels bureaucratic"

**Response:**
- DCF is a thinking approach, not a checklist
- If it feels like overhead, you're over-applying
- Match engagement to stakes

### "Not everyone wants to adopt"

**Response:**
- Don't force adoption
- Let champions demonstrate value
- Make it easy to try, easy to skip

### "We're not seeing benefits"

**Response:**
- Are you measuring the right things?
- Are you applying DCF to appropriate tasks?
- Consider whether practices need adjustment

## Adoption Metrics

### Leading Indicators (Early)
- Number of people trying DCF practices
- Frequency of checkpoint questions in reviews
- Quality of prompts in AI interactions

### Lagging Indicators (Later)
- Reduction in rework/revision cycles
- Improved code review quality
- Better decision documentation
- Team satisfaction with AI tools

## Quick Start Checklist

For teams who want to start immediately:

- [ ] Identify 1-2 champions
- [ ] Share DCF Essentials with champions
- [ ] Champions practice for 2 weeks
- [ ] Introduce one question team-wide: "What assumptions are in this?"
- [ ] After 1 month, assess: Is this helping?
- [ ] If yes, expand. If no, diagnose why.
