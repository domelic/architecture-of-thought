# DCF Research Agenda

Open questions and research opportunities for advancing the Dialectical Cognition Framework.

---

## Overview

DCF is built on theoretical foundations and practical intuition, but many questions remain empirically untested. This agenda outlines research priorities for validating, refining, and extending the framework.

---

## Priority 1: Core Validation

### 1.1 Does DCF Actually Work?

**Question:** Do practitioners using DCF produce better outcomes than those using standard AI interaction patterns?

**Study Design:**
- Randomized controlled trial
- Groups: DCF-trained vs. untrained vs. prompt-engineering-trained
- Tasks: Complex problem-solving, code review, strategic decisions
- Measures: Output quality, time to completion, error rates, learning outcomes

**Metrics:**
- Blind expert rating of outputs
- Objective measures (test pass rates, bug counts)
- Participant self-assessment
- Knowledge retention after delay

**Challenges:**
- Defining "DCF-trained" consistently
- Controlling for individual differences
- Ecological validity (lab vs. real work)

---

### 1.2 Which Components Matter Most?

**Question:** Which DCF principles contribute most to effectiveness?

**Study Design:**
- Ablation study: systematically remove DCF components
- Compare: Full DCF vs. Socratic only vs. Refinement only vs. Checkpoint only
- Measure: Relative contribution of each component

**Hypotheses to Test:**
- H1: Socratic questioning is sufficient without other components
- H2: Recursive refinement is the key mechanism
- H3: Components are synergistic (full DCF > sum of parts)

---

### 1.3 When Does DCF Help vs. Hurt?

**Question:** Under what conditions is DCF beneficial, neutral, or counterproductive?

**Variables to Manipulate:**
- Task complexity (simple → complex)
- Time pressure (low → high)
- Domain familiarity (novice → expert)
- Stakes (exploratory → consequential)

**Expected Pattern:**
- DCF helps more with complex, high-stakes, unfamiliar tasks
- DCF may hurt under extreme time pressure for simple tasks

## Priority 2: Mechanism Studies

### 2.1 What Cognitive Processes Does DCF Engage?

**Question:** What happens cognitively when people apply DCF?

**Methods:**
- Think-aloud protocols during DCF interactions
- Eye-tracking on AI outputs
- Cognitive load measures (dual-task paradigm)
- fMRI/EEG for neural correlates (exploratory)

**Constructs to Measure:**
- Metacognitive monitoring
- Critical evaluation
- Assumption identification
- Mental model updating

---

### 2.2 Extended Mind Hypothesis Test

**Question:** Does AI become functionally integrated into cognition under DCF?

**Criteria (from Clark & Chalmers):**
1. Constant availability
2. Direct access
3. Automatic endorsement
4. Past endorsement

**Study Design:**
- Compare experienced DCF practitioners vs. novices
- Measure: Speed of integration, trust calibration, cognitive offloading patterns
- Look for: Differences in how AI is treated cognitively

**Prediction:** Experienced DCF practitioners show patterns consistent with extended cognition; novices don't.

---

### 2.3 Scaffolding and Fading

**Question:** Does DCF use lead to capability building or dependency?

**Longitudinal Study:**
- Track practitioners over 6-12 months
- Periodically test: Ability to perform similar tasks without AI
- Measure: Skill transfer, cognitive atrophy, independence

**Key Question:** Does the "scaffolding" actually fade, or does dependency grow?

## Priority 3: Application Studies

### 3.1 Domain Specificity

**Question:** How should DCF be adapted for different domains?

**Domains to Study:**
- Software development
- Scientific research
- Business strategy
- Creative writing
- Legal analysis
- Medical diagnosis support

**For Each Domain:**
- What modifications improve DCF effectiveness?
- Which principles are universal vs. domain-specific?
- What domain-specific anti-patterns emerge?

---

### 3.2 Team Applications

**Question:** How does DCF work in team contexts?

**Research Questions:**
- Should teams share DCF practices?
- How do power dynamics affect DCF effectiveness?
- What rituals support team DCF adoption?
- Does DCF improve or complicate team decision-making?

**Study Design:**
- Compare: Individual DCF vs. team DCF vs. team without DCF
- Tasks: Group problem-solving, collaborative design
- Measures: Output quality, team dynamics, individual learning

---

### 3.3 Educational Applications

**Question:** Can DCF be taught effectively? What's the learning curve?

**Research Questions:**
- What's the minimum effective DCF training?
- Does explicit instruction outperform learning by doing?
- What prior knowledge/skills predict DCF adoption success?
- At what age/skill level can DCF be introduced?

## Priority 4: Measurement Development

### 4.1 DCF Proficiency Assessment

**Need:** Validated instrument to measure DCF skill

**Components:**
- Socratic questioning ability
- Metacognitive awareness during AI use
- Refinement skill
- Assumption identification
- Trust calibration

**Development Process:**
1. Item generation from theory
2. Expert review
3. Pilot testing
4. Factor analysis
5. Validity studies

---

### 4.2 Synergy Index Validation

**Need:** Validate the proposed human-AI synergy metrics

**Questions:**
- Are the four dimensions (task completion, innovation, adaptability, error detection) distinct?
- Do they predict meaningful outcomes?
- Can they be measured reliably?
- How do they relate to DCF use?

---

### 4.3 Interaction Quality Coding Scheme

**Need:** Systematic way to evaluate DCF application quality

**Development:**
- Create coding manual for AI interaction transcripts
- Train coders
- Establish inter-rater reliability
- Correlate with outcomes

### 5.1 DCF and AI Alignment

**Question:** How does DCF relate to AI alignment research?

**Intersections:**
- Does human-in-the-loop via DCF reduce alignment failures?

**Question:** Does DCF apply beyond LLMs?

**Extensions to Study:**
- DCF with multimodal AI

## Research Methodology Notes

### Recommended Approaches

**Quantitative:**
- Randomized controlled trials for intervention effects
- Longitudinal designs for development/dependency questions
- Psychometric methods for instrument development

**Qualitative:**
- Think-aloud protocols for process understanding
- Grounded theory for emergent patterns
- Case studies for domain-specific applications

**Mixed Methods:**
- Combine outcome measurement with process tracing
- Use qualitative findings to refine quantitative measures

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Demand characteristics**: Participants trying to "prove DCF works"
2. **Confounding with AI familiarity**: DCF training may include AI exposure
3. **Hawthorne effects**: Any intervention may improve outcomes
4. **Ecological validity**: Lab tasks may not transfer to real work
5. **Publication bias**: Null results are important to publish
