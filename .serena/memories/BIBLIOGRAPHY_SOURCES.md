## Sources by Topic

### Extended Mind and Distributed Cognition

| Key | Author(s) | Work | Use In Document |
|-----|-----------|------|-----------------|
| `clark1998extended` | Clark & Chalmers | "The Extended Mind" (1998) | **Foundational** - Thinking Mirror hypothesis derives from this |
| `clark2008supersizing` | Clark | *Supersizing the Mind* (2008) | Extended treatment of cognitive extension |
| `clark2015surfing` | Clark | *Surfing Uncertainty* (2015) | Predictive processing, relevant to anticipatory calibration |
| `hutchins1995cognition` | Hutchins | *Cognition in the Wild* (1995) | **Foundational** - Distributed cognition, system-level analysis |

**When to cite**: Any discussion of cognition extending beyond individual minds, human-AI systems as cognitive units.

## Adding New References

### BibTeX Entry Format
```bibtex
@article{authorYYYYkeyword,
  author = {Last, First and Last2, First2},
  title = {Title Here},
  journal = {Journal Name},
  volume = {XX},
  number = {N},
  pages = {XX--YY},
  year = {YYYY},
  doi = {10.xxxx/xxxxx}
}
```

### Placement in references.bib
Add to the appropriate section (marked by comment headers like `% EXTENDED MIND`).
