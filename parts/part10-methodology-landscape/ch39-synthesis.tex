\chapter{Synthesis: Why All Layers Matter}

The most effective practitioners operate across all layers:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{MACRO}: Use frameworks like Ralph Loop to structure when AI acts autonomously vs. when you intervene
    \item \textbf{MESO}: Use patterns like Research-Plan-Implement to organize complex projects
    \item \textbf{MICRO}: Use Socratic dialogue and recursive refinement to think effectively in each interaction
    \item \textbf{ATOMIC}: Craft individual prompts well using established techniques
\end{enumerate}

\section{The Unique Contribution of This Treatise}

Most frameworks focus on \emph{what the AI should do}. This treatise focuses on \emph{how the human should think}. It fills a gap in the ecosystem:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Ralph tells AI how to behave between checkpoints
    \item Plan Mode structures phases of work
    \item \textbf{DCF tells you how to think at the checkpoints, during the phases, and throughout the collaboration}
\end{itemize}

It's the cognitive operating system that runs on top of whatever agentic framework you choose.
