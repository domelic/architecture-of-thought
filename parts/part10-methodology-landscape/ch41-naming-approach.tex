\chapter{Naming the Approach}

This methodology needs a name. The recommended name is:

\section{The Dialectical Cognition Framework (DCF)}

This name captures:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Dialectical}: The back-and-forth nature of productive human-AI dialogue
    \item \textbf{Cognition}: The focus on thinking, not just task completion
    \item \textbf{Framework}: The structured, teachable nature of the approach
\end{itemize}

For casual use: ``\textbf{The Thinking Mirror Approach}''

The name matters less than the practice. But having a name enables teaching it to others, discussing it in teams, positioning it alongside other methodologies, and building community around it.
