\chapter{Practical Integration}

\section{Using Socratic Prompting within Ralph Loop}

\begin{lstlisting}
[Ralph working autonomously on a task...]
[Checkpoint reached]

Human (Socratic): "Before I approve this direction, help me understand:
- What assumptions are you making?
- What alternatives did you consider?
- What could go wrong?"

[AI responds with analysis]

Human (Recursive Refinement): "Given those concerns, let's revise
the approach. What would change if we prioritized X over Y?"

[Continue until clarity achieved, then approve]
\end{lstlisting}

\section{Using Recursive Refinement in Plan Mode}

\begin{lstlisting}
[In "Plan" phase]

AI: "Here's my proposed implementation plan..."

Human (Socratic): "Before we commit to this plan:
- What would a simpler solution look like?
- What's the riskiest assumption here?
- How would we know if this plan is wrong?"

[Iterate 2-3 times, refining the plan]

Human (Metacognitive): "What would I miss if I just approved the
first plan? This iteration revealed X, Y, Z--worth the extra cycles."
\end{lstlisting}
