\chapter{The Future of Thought Work}

The trajectory is clear:

\section{Near Term (1--3 years)}

\begin{itemize}
    \item LLM integration becomes standard in knowledge work
    \item Companies recognize the need for AI literacy
    \item Methodologies like this one become competitive advantages
\end{itemize}

\section{Medium Term (3--7 years)}

\begin{itemize}
    \item AI-augmented thinking becomes baseline expectation
    \item New roles emerge: cognitive systems architect, AI integration specialist
    \item Education incorporates AI collaboration skills
\end{itemize}

\section{Long Term (7+ years)}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Human-AI collaboration becomes seamless
    \item The distinction between ``AI-assisted'' and ``unassisted'' work fades
    \item New forms of thought become possible that neither human nor AI could achieve alone
\end{itemize}
