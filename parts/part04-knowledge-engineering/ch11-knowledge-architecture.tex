\chapter{Knowledge Architecture}

Beyond individual documents lies \textbf{knowledge architecture}: the structure of how documentation interconnects, evolves, and scales.

Principles of knowledge architecture:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Hierarchy}: Concepts nest within concepts
    \item \textbf{Cross-reference}: Related ideas link explicitly
    \item \textbf{Versioning}: Knowledge evolves; history matters
    \item \textbf{Discoverability}: Users can find what they need
    \item \textbf{Maintainability}: Updates propagate correctly
\end{enumerate}

LLMs can assist with:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Generating suggested links between documents
    \item Identifying orphaned or outdated content
    \item Creating consistent taxonomies
    \item Proposing structural reorganizations
\end{itemize}
