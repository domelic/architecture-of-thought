\chapter{Extended Thinking and Internal Dialectic}

Modern Claude models feature ``extended thinking''---internal reasoning that happens before response generation. This is DCF happening \emph{inside the model}.

\section{What Extended Thinking Means for DCF}

The model now:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Considers multiple approaches internally
    \item Argues with itself about trade-offs
    \item Identifies potential issues before surfacing them
    \item Converges on answers through internal iteration
\end{itemize}

\section{Your Role Shifts}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Less need to prompt for ``think step by step''
    \item More need to evaluate the \emph{output} of that thinking
    \item Focus on whether the model's reasoning aligns with your goals
    \item Challenge conclusions, not process
\end{itemize}

\section{Requesting Visibility}

\begin{quote}
Human: ``Show me your reasoning for this recommendation. What did you consider and reject?''
\end{quote}

This surfaces the internal dialectic, making it available for your review.
