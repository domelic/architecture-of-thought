\section{DCF Principles Translated}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Thinking Mirror} → The agent's \emph{plan} is now the mirror. Review it Socratically before approval.
    \item \textbf{Socratic Dialogue} → Use at checkpoints: ``Before I approve this, what assumptions are you making?''
    \item \textbf{Recursive Refinement} → The agent does this internally; your job is to evaluate whether convergence is correct.
    \item \textbf{Metacognition} → Monitor your own cognitive engagement. Are you rubber-stamping, or genuinely reviewing?
\end{enumerate}
